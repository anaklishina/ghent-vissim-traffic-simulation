## Study-specific caveats

- The bridge/quay split of AWV approach E is an assumption (60/40 for motor and bicycle demand), not a measured origin allocation.
- Maximum recorded queues may be affected by the network's limited upstream extent.
- The original baseline network and increased-demand model were not fully identical in upstream layout, so changes in queue metrics cannot be attributed solely to demand.
- The +20% scenario result workbook is retained, but its original standalone INPX configuration was not archived.
- The detector-actuated tram control is an alternative exploratory strategy rather than the official AWV tram-control sequence.
- Results should not be used for infrastructure or signal-control decisions without further data and validation.
